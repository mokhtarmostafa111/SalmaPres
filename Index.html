Zoho Contracts + Sign · 05
Contract Obligations

Task obligations

Actions someone must complete
One-time: the customer pays the invoice by the due date.
Recurring: a monthly service report. Once marked done, the next one is created automatically.

Non-task obligations

Rules that apply for the whole contract
  • Confidentiality of shared information
  • Protection of customer data
  • No hiring of the other party’s employees
Obligations of both parties are recorded in the contract and assigned to internal owners, with reminders before due dates and progress tracked across contracts.

1
2
3
4

Request received

The customer gets the signature request from Zoho Sign.

OTP is sent

A one-time password (OTP) is sent to the customer.

Identity verified

The customer enters the OTP before the document opens.

Review and sign

Only then can the customer view and sign the contract.

The contract stays protected: only the intended signer can open and sign it.
